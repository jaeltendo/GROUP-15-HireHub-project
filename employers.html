For Employers Page

This page is for employers who want to hire workers.

How employers use HireHub

First, the employer creates an account. Then the employer posts a job. Job seekers see the job and apply. Last, the employer checks the applications and chooses the best worker.

Information Employers Provide

  • Company name
  • Job title
  • Location
  • Salary
  • Job description
  • Requirements

How Employers Find Skilled Workers

Employers can check the applications. They can also search for workers by skill. For example, if they need a plumber, they search for plumbers and contact them.
