Job Seeker Tips

A little preparation can help you make a clear first impression.

Prepare your details

Keep your phone number current and list skills that match the work you want.

Describe your experience

Include informal work, volunteering, training, and practical projects where relevant.

Ask about the role

Confirm duties, hours, location, pay, and the employer’s expectations before accepting work.
